The design of Plerni was adversarially reviewed across four independent model families — pseudonymized α (authoring), β, γ, δ per `AGENTS.md` §0 — plus a historical counter-evidence pass against the predecessor repository.

The raw transcripts cite predecessor-internal details (private tracker IDs, internal paths, client-adjacent references), and the specific tooling used in the maintainer's process is private — both are **retained privately** per the repository's public-data policy in `AGENTS.md` §0. `SHA256SUMS.txt` in this directory is the integrity manifest: each transcript's hash under its pseudonymized name, so the private archive can be verified against what the public design claims. Note the limit: hashes prove integrity, not review quality or independence — with pseudonymized identities the public can verify an accountable assertion and its scope, not independently establish family separation.
